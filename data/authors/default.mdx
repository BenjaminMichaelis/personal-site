Benjamin Michaelis is a product-minded software engineer at [IntelliTect](https://intellitect.com/), where he builds reliable web and cloud systems, developer tools, and full-stack .NET applications that help teams ship faster. He has contributed to IntelliTect’s products, including [StormingTheCastle.com](https://stormingcastle.com/), and delivered solutions across higher education, utilities, financial services, and startups, from offline-capable clients to serverless pipelines and enterprise integrations.

Benjamin is the primary maintainer of [EssentialCSharp.com](https://essentialcsharp.com/) and co-author of Essential C#. He drives the site’s tooling and content roadmap and recently added AI-powered chat features that surface context-aware documentation and examples. He’s also an active open-source contributor, publishing NuGet packages, GitHub Actions, and developer utilities.

He holds a B.S. in Software Engineering from Washington State University and multiple certifications, including Azure Solutions Architect Expert, Azure Administrator Associate, and GitHub Actions. Beyond building software, Benjamin teaches the .NET programming course at Eastern Washington University, mentors new developers, and guest lectures at events across the Pacific Northwest.

Outside of code, he enjoys outdoor adventures, photography, traveling, and staying active with friends and family.
